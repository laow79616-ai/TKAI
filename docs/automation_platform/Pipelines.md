# Pipelines

Pipelines declare Sequential, Parallel, or Conditional intent and provide retry,
rollback, checkpoint, and timeout controls. Actions integrate workflows, agents,
applications, notifications, approvals, script interfaces, connectors, models,
and knowledge services through registered handlers.

Conditions support boolean, expression, threshold, state, time, dependency, and
custom evaluation. History captures attempts, failures, checkpoints, results,
approvals, completion, and duration.
