# Campaign Center Operations Guide

1. Create a Draft campaign with a tenant, workspace, owner, and objective.
2. Add scoped content and execution references plus a schedule.
3. Move the campaign through Planning and Review.
4. Record an authorized, unexpired approval.
5. Move to Approved and Scheduled.
6. Move to Running only after checking monitoring and risk status.
7. Pause on restrictions, health failures, or approval invalidation.
8. Complete, archive, and optionally soft-delete according to retention policy.

Use `/tiktok/campaigns/dashboard` for the overview and
`/tiktok/campaigns/metrics` for Prometheus text. History views preserve status,
approval, execution, and audit events.
