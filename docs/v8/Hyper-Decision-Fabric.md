# Hyper Decision Fabric

The fabric standardizes profiles, decisions, alternatives, comparisons,
recommendations, evaluations, confidence, evidence, reviews, approvals, and
compatibility. Recommendations and approvals are descriptive metadata only.

Decision profiles contain ownership, version, health, metrics, audit, and references
to decisions, alternatives, evidence, knowledge, reasoning, recommendations,
governance, and compatibility. Decision metadata includes classification, safe
explainability summaries, confidence, review, approval, evaluation, and version
history references. Executable decision content is rejected.
