# Operations Guide

1. Register the Command Center and its control planes.
2. Add platform adapters as topology nodes and verify synchronization.
3. Record health checks and validate the health map.
4. Route alerts to incident and playbook workflows.
5. Require approval identifiers for destructive lifecycle or playbook actions.
6. Monitor Command Center metrics and activity for failures or capacity limits.
7. During recovery, execute the rollback plan, record resolution and recovery,
   and attach root-cause and postmortem references.
8. Archive only after operations, alerts, incidents, and audit exports complete.
