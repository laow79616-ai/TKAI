# TKAI Business Platform V2 Operator Guide

Use `scripts/status-all.ps1`, `scripts/health-check.ps1`, and
`scripts/smoke-test.ps1` for routine checks. Create account, browser, proxy, content,
and task metadata with mock or authorized references only. Every task requires an
explicit policy, permission, approval state, and audit trail before any existing
authorized execution service may consume its reference. Stop on a restriction,
challenge, authorization uncertainty, or suspected secret exposure.
